The Ghoul

For many years there have been whisperings of something...

Known as The Ghoul, this mysterious being has been spotted lurking in the shadows. He only makes his presence known at night whole he searches. Searches for something, but nobody is able to figure out exactly what that something is.
Nobody has really been able to get a good look at him, but they feel his presence. A deep longing to belong, and a strong desire to not be cast out as a freak.
